## 🎙️ Sentiment Read
The dominant signal right now is **hawkish, not dovish** — an unusual posture for a "Fed Watch" brief and worth flagging explicitly. Three concrete anchors:

1. **The July dissent.** Three regional Fed presidents voted for a hike rather than a hold in July — a rare, sizeable hawkish dissent that JPMorgan Wealth Management strategists say has "lowered the bar" for a September move.
2. **Warsh's Jackson Hole language (Aug 28).** Chair Warsh said better-than-expected summer inflation readings did not show underlying trends had "meaningfully improved," and that the Fed needs confidence inflation is moving toward its 2% objective "clearly and quickly enough." That specific language is what moved CME FedWatch odds from ~30% to ~56% probability of a hike almost overnight.
3. **Inflation still running well above target.** Both CPI (3.4%/2.5% y/y) and core PCE (3.3% y/y) remain more than a percentage point above the Fed's 2% goal, which Warsh has repeatedly noted has been missed for over five years.

Layered on top: escalating U.S.-Iran military conflict (see below) is pushing oil and headline inflation risk higher just as the Fed enters its blackout period, removing any chance of fresh verbal guidance before the meeting.
