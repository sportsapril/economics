## 🎙️ Sentiment Read

This is a hawkish signal, anchored in specifics rather than tone. Three regional Fed presidents dissented in favor of a hike — a rare and pointedly hawkish three-way split, and the first time in this cycle multiple members have pushed for tightening rather than easing. Chair Warsh's explicit rejection of a "soft inflation target" and his description of the current setting as "no soft implicit target... only a target, and it's 2%" reinforces that the Committee leadership is not signaling tolerance for above-target inflation, even with a Q2 GDP slowdown to 1.5% and a soft June jobs report (+57K, well below consensus). The bond market's reaction — 10-year and 30-year yields jumping while the 2-year slipped — reads as the market questioning the Fed's inflation-fighting credibility (a "bear steepener") rather than expressing confidence in the hold. The FedWatch swing from a 54% cut probability to an 81% hike probability in two trading days is the starkest evidence that markets read this meeting as hawkish, not dovish.

This rally is earnings-driven rather than Fed-driven, and shows equities are currently being pulled in two directions — hawkish Fed repricing versus resilient AI-driven corporate earnings. *(fact, with the caveat that the driver is separate from Fed policy)*
